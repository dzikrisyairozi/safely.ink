## What safely.ink is

safely.ink is a policy fuzzer. You upload a policy or contract, and it turns the wording into rules, tests those rules against many realistic scenarios, and shows where you may end up worse off than a reasonable person would expect, or worse off than the law allows. For documents from Indonesia, Singapore and Australia, it also compares the deciding rules with the law. The "How it works" page explains each step.

## An information tool, not legal advice

safely.ink gives you information about the wording of a document. It does not give legal, financial or other professional advice, and a report is not a legal opinion. It does not draft, rewrite or negotiate documents, and it does not contact the other party or act for you.

Before you make an important decision, such as signing, cancelling, claiming or going to court, check the clauses yourself and talk to a qualified professional or the relevant regulator.

## How accurate the results are

Please read this part carefully.

- **Reading can be wrong.** Language models read the document and propose the rules and scenarios. They can misread a clause, miss one, or describe it imperfectly. Every rule links to its clause so you can check.
- **Outcomes follow the extracted rules.** The outcome of each scenario is calculated by code, the same way every time, but it is only as right as the rules it was given.
- **Scenarios are examples.** They are realistic situations, not every situation. A document with few traps in the report can still hurt you in a case that was not tested.
- **Some clauses cannot be tested.** Discretion and vague wording are listed as unclear, not decided. Read those clauses yourself.
- **Legal comparison is limited.** It covers Indonesia, Singapore and Australia only, and searches a fixed list of official sources. A citation is shown only when its quote was found on the official page we fetched. "No finding" means we found nothing we could verify. It does not mean the law allows the clause. Laws change, and a page we fetched may be out of date.
- **The grade is a summary.** It comes from a fixed formula over the traps found, and the report shows the formula. It is not a rating of the company or its products.

## Neutral voice

Reports describe what the wording of a document does in particular situations. They never say a company is dishonest, and they do not rank companies. Please use them in the same spirit.
